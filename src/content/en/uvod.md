On 27 August we set off together on our Silver expedition. We met in the morning at Prague main station and took the train to Veselí nad Lužnicí. Then we changed to a bus and rode on to Jindřichův Hradec, where Maru joined us.

So there were five of us in total: me (Sofi), Maru, Tobi, Pavel and our expedition leader Štěpán. In Jindřichův Hradec we also had our last shop-bought meal from the COOP, which was a bit makeshift but good :). Then we caught the last bus to Nová Bystřice, where our expedition could really begin.
